# Collective Image Lab · 5AAVC200

Images generated by students during the Digital Methods Labs.

## Licence
Images are donated by students under CC BY 4.0. Reuse with attribution to
"5AAVC200 Digital Methods, King's College London".

Donating is separate from the lab exercise — every student completes the
exercise whether or not they contribute images here. To have an image
removed, contact the module convenor; it is withdrawn at the next rebuild
and its position is not reused.

## Reading the views
Similarity arranges by visual similarity computed from colour and
composition. Hue and Light arrange by average hue and lightness. Name
arranges by filename. The same images, read four ways.
